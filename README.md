A mod menu for CarX Drift Racing Online 2! Contains (as of right now) fly mode (a safe ghost mode, so nobody can hit you, toggled with F6), reduced bumper colliders (similar to Kino mod in DRO1), custom tags that show on your name and that other Prevail users can see, team icons and tags on player names, real car names, a Team tab for approved teams (plus team requests), and settings for keybinds and quality-of-life options like muting drift point sounds! Updates frequently and very open to suggestions.
